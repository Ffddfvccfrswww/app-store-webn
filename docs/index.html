    Questions

    Before the lights go down

    What is ZARKOR?

    ZARKOR is an online showcase where app publishers pay a monthly fee to present their apps to visitors. Think of it as a small cinema for apps: a curated programme, presented with care.

    How do I get my app listed?

    Choose a ticket, fill in the short submission form and we will review your app. Once it is approved and payment is completed, your poster goes live.

    How does billing work?

    Plans are billed monthly in advance through a secure third-party payment provider. We never see or store your card details.

    Can I cancel?

    Yes, at any time. Your listing stays online until the end of the period you have already paid for, and you will not be charged again.

    Do you offer refunds?

    Our Refund Policy explains when refunds apply, including the withdrawal rights that consumers have in some countries. It is linked at the bottom of every page.

    Which apps do you accept?

    Lawful apps that are available to the public and follow our Content Guidelines. We do not list apps that are deceptive, malicious, hateful or sexually explicit, or that target children unlawfully. We may decline or remove a listing at our discretion.

    Do you guarantee downloads?

    No, and you should be careful with anyone who does. We sell visibility on our stage, not results. How many people install your app depends on the app, the audience and many things outside our control.

    Are listings marked as advertising?

    Always. Every paid placement shows a “Sponsored” label so visitors know exactly what they are looking at.

    Do you track visitors?

    No. We do not use advertising or profiling cookies. The site remembers only your language, motion and privacy choices, and it keeps them on your own device.

    Which languages are supported?

    The site is available in English, Hebrew, Arabic, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese and Japanese. You can provide your listing text in any of them.
